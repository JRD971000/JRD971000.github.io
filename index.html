I am a researcher at NVIDIA in the Deep Learning Efficiency Research group, led by Pavlo Molchanov. My current work focuses on improving the efficiency of large language models using model compression, structured pruning, distillation, quantization, speculation, and elastic multi-budget inference.

I contribute to NVIDIA model families and tooling including Nemotron, NeMo Framework, Megatron-LM, Megatron Bridge, BioNeMo, ModelOpt, TensorRT-LLM, and vLLM. Recent work includes Nemotron Nano 2, Minitron-SSM, Star Elastic, Evo 2, and EDEN.

Prior to joining NVIDIA full-time, I completed my Ph.D. at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Matthew West. My Ph.D. work used graph neural networks and reinforcement learning to accelerate scientific computing methods such as algebraic multigrid and domain decomposition solvers.
